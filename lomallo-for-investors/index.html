Nine years of travel experience, now powered by modern booking technology.

Market & problem

Lomallo is entering a €618B European travel market with visible customer pain.

Travel buying is fragmented. Multiple intermediaries add cost, small and medium-sized companies lack efficient travel management tools, and customers often see many prices for the same hotel without knowing the real market price.

EU travel spend€618B

A large market where even narrow penetration can create meaningful scale.

Why the model matters

  • Recurring monthly and annual fees create predictable revenue
  • Booking activity grows commission income as usage increases
  • Ownership sales add cash flow alongside platform growth
  • The value promise is simple: more choice, less effort and lower prices

Competitive advantage

Better price, broad supply and real service create a differentiated customer experience.

Lomallo’s advantage comes from a combination customers can easily understand: wide selection, competitive prices and personal assistance. The market has many self-service channels, but Lomallo adds support on top of savings, making the service attractive for both companies and consumers.

Revenue streams

Three revenue streams make the business stronger and less dependent on one source.

Lomallo combines paid customer relationships, booking commissions and ownership-based accommodation sales. This gives the company several ways to monetize the same customer relationship and creates a more resilient model than a pure booking platform.

1

Recurring fees

Monthly and annual customer fees are expected to form the largest revenue stream.

2

Booking commissions

Accommodation bookings generate 4-20% commission income depending on service level.

3

Ownership business

Holiday accommodation share sales complement the service business with 40-60% sales margin potential.

Strategy

Growth is built step by step, based on existing proof.

Lomallo’s strategy starts with increasing usage and sales in Finland, where the company already has a customer base, operating experience and market knowledge. Once the model is strong in the home market, growth can be accelerated through digital customer acquisition, corporate sales and partnerships.

Growth sequence

  • Commercial scaling from direct sales to digital customer acquisition
  • Continuous technology development to secure competitive advantage
  • New benefits and partnerships to increase customer value
  • International scaling after the home base is proven
