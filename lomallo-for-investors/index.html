Investment case

Nine years of travel experience, now powered by modern booking technology.

Lomallo has served companies, entrepreneurs and families with cost-efficient travel and personal customer service for nine years. The investment case is about turning that customer understanding into a scalable platform model with international potential.

1

Proven service demand

Lomallo already has a long operating history in helping customers travel more cost-effectively through a high-service model.

2

Modern platform launch

In March 2026 Lomallo launched an intelligent booking system that brings the business from personal service toward a scalable technology platform.

3

Massive accommodation supply

The platform gives customers access to 3.2 million properties with a price position designed to be among the most competitive in the market.

Market & problem

Lomallo is entering a €618B European travel market with visible customer pain.

Travel buying is fragmented. Multiple intermediaries add cost, small and medium-sized companies lack efficient travel management tools, and customers often see many prices for the same hotel without knowing the real market price.

Pricing

Clear pricing lowers the threshold to buy and creates a route to recurring revenue.

Lomallo’s pricing is structured so that customers can start easily and upgrade as the value becomes visible. Paid customer tiers turn savings into concrete customer value, while also supporting acquisition, conversion and recurring billing.

€0 entry tier for low-friction customer acquisition

Why the model matters for investors

  • Recurring monthly and annual fees create predictable revenue
  • Customer loyalty increases as benefits improve with customer level
  • The business shifts from individual reservations toward ongoing customer relationships
  • Better cash flow and scalability can support a more valuable company
  • The value promise is simple: more choice, less effort and lower prices

Revenue streams

Three revenue streams make the business stronger and less dependent on one source.

Lomallo combines paid customer relationships, booking commissions and ownership-based accommodation sales. This gives the company several ways to monetize the same customer relationship and creates a more resilient business model than a pure booking platform.

1

Recurring fees

Monthly and annual customer fees are expected to form the largest revenue stream and make revenue more predictable.

2

Booking commissions

Accommodation bookings generate commission income of 4-20%, depending on the customer’s service level.

3

Ownership business

Sales of holiday accommodation shares complement the service business and add another growth engine.

Ownership model

Lomallo Ownership can bring cash flow alongside platform growth.

Lomallo does not build the future only on a digital service. The ownership model buys destinations at market price or below and sells them forward either as fractional ownership or as complete units. The model supports cash flow while the digital service scales.

Purchase price At or below market

Destinations are acquired with price discipline.

Exit format Shares or whole units

Assets can be sold as fractional ownership or complete holiday units.

Sales margin 40-60%

The deck indicates that the sales margin remains with the company.

Strategic benefit Flexibility

The model adds security and flexibility while supporting the service business.
