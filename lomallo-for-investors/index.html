Investment case

This is not a cold-start travel idea. It is a tested business entering its platform phase.

Lomallo has real customers, a proven service history and a fresh booking technology layer. The interesting moment is now: the company is moving from manual service and direct sales toward a platform model that can scale customer acquisition, pricing power and recurring revenue.

Market opening

A large market with a simple frustration: travel is still too expensive and too fragmented.

Customers often face multiple intermediaries, opaque hotel prices and too much comparison work. Small and medium-sized companies also lack an efficient, human-backed travel tool. Lomallo’s opportunity is to make better-priced travel easier to buy and easier to repeat.

Growth path

First prove Finland, then scale the model internationally.

The next phase is deliberately staged. Lomallo first grows usage and sales in Finland, where it already has customers, experience and market understanding. After that, digital acquisition, corporate sales and partnerships can support expansion to the Nordics and wider Europe.

What must work

  • Direct sales evolves into repeatable digital acquisition
  • Technology development improves conversion and customer value
  • Benefits and partnerships make paid tiers more attractive
  • SuperStays adds a sharper product story for selected investor discussions
