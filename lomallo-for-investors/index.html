A new era of travel for companies and consumers.

Lomallo is a Finnish growth company building a scalable, profitable and international travel platform. The company combines nine years of travel service experience with a new intelligent booking system launched in March 2026.

Investment case

Nine years of travel experience, now powered by modern booking technology.

Lomallo has served companies, entrepreneurs and families with cost-efficient travel and personal customer service for nine years. The investment case is about turning that customer understanding into a scalable platform model with international potential.

1

Proven service demand

Lomallo already has a long operating history in helping customers travel more cost-effectively through a high-service model.

2

Modern platform launch

In March 2026 Lomallo launched an intelligent booking system that brings the business from personal service toward a scalable technology platform.

3

Massive accommodation supply

The platform gives customers access to 3.2 million properties with a price position designed to be among the most competitive in the market.

Business model

Personal travel service meets intelligent booking technology.

The new booking system expands Lomallo from a service-led travel club into a technology-enabled travel platform for companies and consumers. The model keeps the trusted customer relationship while making supply, pricing and booking flows scalable.
