Investor introduction

A membership travel company built around access, savings and unique stays.

Lomallo gives companies and their people access to curated holiday properties, exclusive use-rights and hands-on travel service. The model combines recurring membership revenue, negotiated travel benefits and a growing portfolio of distinctive destinations.

2026 target base 1,500 corporate customers
Users 10,000 travelers in the network
Annual trips 13,000 business and leisure trips arranged

Investment case

Lomallo is positioned between corporate travel service, member benefits and destination ownership.

The opportunity is to scale a travel membership model where customers save time and money, while Lomallo builds defensible supply through owned destinations, exclusive sales rights and negotiated accommodation capacity.

1

Clear customer value

Members gain access to unique destinations at predictable use fees, without the responsibilities, risks or administration of ownership.

2

Supply that differentiates

Lomallo combines its own properties, exclusive-use arrangements and a broad external inventory into one high-service travel offering.

3

Scalable commercial platform

A growing corporate customer base can be monetized through memberships, destination usage, partner sales and selected real estate-backed opportunities.

Business model

A travel club with recurring customer relationships and proprietary demand.

Lomallo has evolved from a travel club for corporate customers into a broader platform for better leisure travel. The company serves customers with personal assistance, exclusive destination access and negotiated pricing.

Founded roots
Travel professionals building a closed travel club since 2017
Customer relationship
Companies join the service and extend access to selected users
Core promise
Better holidays, less booking work and meaningful savings

Where revenue can grow

  • Membership and joining fees
  • Use fees from Lomallo-owned destinations
  • Commission and margin from travel bookings
  • Exclusive sales rights and partner inventory
  • Selected asset-backed destination investments

Portfolio logic

Unique destinations create a tangible reason to join and stay.

Lomallo’s offering includes domestic flagship properties, exclusive sales-right destinations, international apartments and a large global accommodation inventory. The strongest destinations work as customer acquisition assets as much as holiday products.

Lomallo-owned destinations

Properties where members can book at defined use fees, often below comparable market pricing.

Exclusive sales-right properties

High-quality partner destinations where Lomallo can differentiate through availability and price.

Curated partner supply

A wider global inventory makes the service useful beyond the flagship destinations.
